Tap Mode: tap the pads.
Motion Mode: swing your phone (or a stick holding it) like a drumstick.
Swing direction picks the drum: down=snare, up=hi-hat, left=tom, right=kick.

iPhone no sound? Flip the silent switch on the side off, or turn your volume up.
