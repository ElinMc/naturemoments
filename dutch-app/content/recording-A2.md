# Recording list A2 - kletsen 1 to 10 (Belgian Dutch)

Standard Belgian Dutch as spoken in Flanders. Record the **Nederlands** column only, in order, two seconds of silence between lines. (u) = formal register. (vraag) = question, rising intonation. (TT) = tussentaal, said the way people really say it. Klets 1 is a bridge that recycles A1 fast.
