## Strand 1 - Being sociable (Gezellig)

### Real situations
Meeting a neighbour on the stairs; the first day at a new office; a kringverjaardag; the Friday vrijmibo or borrel; being invited for coffee; texting to arrange or cancel; joining a sportvereniging and doing bardienst; the buurt-WhatsApp; a dinner where everyone gives opinions bluntly; understanding a Dutch friend's deadpan sarcasm.

### Conventions and unwritten rules
- **Greeting:** three kisses (right, left, right) for friends and family of the opposite sex and between women; handshake plus first name at work. "Hoi" and "Doei" are universal; "Dag" works both ways. Regional: "Moi" (north), "Houdoe" (Brabant).
- **Kringverjaardag:** chairs in a circle; you walk the whole circle shaking hands and saying *gefeliciteerd* to everyone, adding "met je moeder / vader / man / zoon". Coffee and one slice of taart first, then borrel later. Bring a small present; stay two to three hours; guests often leave around the same time.
- **Koffie and the one-biscuit rule:** the koektrommel is passed once, you take one, the lid goes back on. This is *nuchterheid* and *zuinigheid*, not stinginess. Koffietijd is about 10.30 and 15.00.
- **Agenda culture:** spontaneous dropping in is rare. "Even in mijn agenda kijken" is a genuine step, not a brush-off; invitations two to three weeks ahead are normal. Cancelling last minute is a bigger faux pas than declining.
- **Directness:** a plain "nee" needs no excuse; feedback comes without cushioning; this reads as honesty and equality, not hostility. Softeners are particles rather than long polite phrases: **even** (makes a request small and quick), **hoor** (reassuring, friendly), **maar** ("go ahead"), **eens**, **toch**, **nou**.
- **"Doe (even) normaal":** the cultural ideal of not showing off; "Doe maar gewoon, dan doe je al gek genoeg."
- **Borrel and terras:** rounds (*een rondje geven*) in bars; going Dutch is literal - a **Tikkie** for your share arrives, ideally not for under about €4, and you answer "komt in orde" or a thumbs-up.
- **Buren:** introduce yourself when you move in; warn neighbours before a party; parcels are routinely accepted for each other; many streets have a buurtapp.
- **Sportvereniging:** membership implies volunteering (bardienst, driving to away matches, refereeing).

## Strand 2 - Going into shops (Winkelen)

### Real situations
Bakker on Saturday morning; supermarket checkout script; returning bottles at the statiegeldautomaat; market stall by weight; drogist for painkillers versus apotheek for a prescription; trying on and exchanging clothes; the fietsenmaker; returning a faulty item; haggling on Marktplaats.

### Conventions and unwritten rules
- **Checkout script:** "Heeft u een bonuskaart?" then "Wilt u de bon(netje)?" (receipts not printed by default) then "Spaart u zegels?" then "Pinnen?" Many shops are **pin only**; contactless is the norm. Bags cost money: "Tasje erbij?"
- **Statiegeld:** €0.15 on small plastic bottles and cans, €0.25 on large bottles; the machine gives a *bonnetje* you hand in at the till or donate.
- **Bakker and markt:** the shopkeeper asks "Wie is er aan de beurt?" or "Zegt u het maar" and closes with "Anders nog iets?" Bread is *heel* or *half*, *gesneden* or *ongesneden*. Market weights: *een ons* = 100 g, *een pond* = 500 g.
- **Drogist versus apotheek:** Kruidvat and Etos sell over-the-counter medicine; prescriptions only at the apotheek, tied to your huisarts and insurance (eigen risico).
- **Returns:** shops usually give 14 to 30 days *ruilen* with kassabon; a *tegoedbon* rather than cash is legal for change-of-mind returns; faulty goods fall under **wettelijke garantie** - you may choose repair or replacement, refund if neither is possible. Online: 14 days *bedenktijd*.
- **Marktplaats:** bidding is expected; bid at least about half the asking price; offering same-day pickup earns a discount; confirm the agreed price in messages before collecting; pay by Tikkie or cash at pickup.
- Staff say "Zegt u het maar" or "Kan ik u helpen?"; the standard deflection is **"Ik kijk even rond"**.

## Strand 3 - Doing DIY (Klussen)

### Real situations
Finding pluggen for a gipswand at Gamma; asking for hout op maat at the zaagservice; getting verf gemengd; hiring an aanhanger; witten and laminaat leggen before moving in or out; the huurwoning voorinspectie and eindinspectie; a leaking kraan or verstopte afvoer; a tripped groep in the meterkast; phoning a loodgieter and reading an offerte; buying second-hand tools on Marktplaats; checking with the gemeente or VvE before a dakkapel.

### Conventions and unwritten rules
- **Bouwmarkten:** Gamma, Praxis, Karwei and Hornbach **[check group ownership]**. Staff are addressed with u; the opener is "Kunt u me even helpen?" or "Ik zoek ...". Aisles are *gangpaden*.
- **Zaagservice:** only sheet material bought in-store; straight cuts only; about €0.50 a cut, free with the Gamma voordeelpas up to about 10 cuts **[check current terms]**. Online "hout op maat" does shapes and bevel cuts. Karwei and Praxis run comparable services **[check]**.
- **Verfmengservice:** Hornbach over 1 million colours, Karwei about 3,500 including RAL and NCS; **mixed paint cannot be returned**. Karwei standard returns: 30 days with bon.
- **Verhuur:** trailers (aanhanger), behangafstomer, tegelsnijder, vloerschuurmachine; reserve online, pay a *borg*.
- **Huurwoning:** you must return the home in the state described in the *opnamerapport* or *opleveringsrapport* from move-in; at the *voorinspectie* the inspector lists what to do (muren witten, gaten dichten, gordijnrails weghalen); *behangklaar* walls may not be papered; then *eindinspectie* and borg return. Social housing is a woningcorporatie.
- **Koopwoning and nieuwbouw:** oplevering with the aannemer or ontwikkelaar; defects go in the *opleveringsrapport*; Vereniging Eigen Huis finds about 20 defects on average. A **kluswoning** is a fixer-upper sold cheap on condition of renovation. *In de bouw werken* = work in construction; *bouwvak* = the summer builders' holiday.
- **VvE:** flat owners' association; permission needed for anything touching common parts even if *vergunningvrij* for the gemeente. Check the **Omgevingsloket** for an *omgevingsvergunning*; a rear dakkapel is often permit-free within size limits, a front one rarely.
- **Tradespeople:** expect *voorrijkosten* and an *uurtarief*; ask for an *offerte* before agreeing; *meerwerk* must be agreed. Gas smell: national number 0800-9009 **[check]**.
- Meterkast etiquette: resetting a tripped *groep* is normal; anything beyond that is "door een erkend installateur".

## Strand 4 - Parent with children in voortgezet onderwijs (Ouders)

### The system
Basisschool ends in **groep 8**. A *voorlopig schooladvies* comes in January; the **doorstroomtoets** (replaced the Cito-eindtoets from 2023-24) is taken in the first two weeks of February; the *definitief schooladvies* follows in March and can only be **bijgesteld naar boven** if the test scores higher; one national *aanmeldweek* at the end of March. Advice can be single (havo) or double (vmbo-t/havo). Some cities use *loting* and *matching* **[check locally]**.

Tracks: **vmbo** (4 years: basis, kader, gemengd, theoretisch or *mavo*), **havo** (5), **vwo** (6: atheneum, gymnasium with Latin and Greek). First year(s) = **brugklas**; a **dakpanklas** mixes two adjacent levels; *brede brugklas*, *categorale* school. **Onderbouw** = first 2 (vmbo) or 3 (havo/vwo) years; **bovenbouw** the rest. **Profielkeuze** at the end of year 3 havo/vwo: Natuur & Techniek, Natuur & Gezondheid, Economie & Maatschappij, Cultuur & Maatschappij (plus vakkenpakket, guided by the *decaan* and LOB lessons). Vmbo pupils pick a *profiel* in year 2 **[check]**.

Assessment: grades 1 to 10, one decimal; **5.5 rounds to 6 = voldoende**; below 5.5 = onvoldoende; 7 ruim voldoende, 8 goed, 9 zeer goed, 10 uitmuntend; "een zesje" = scraping by. Tests: **SO** (schriftelijke overhoring, small), **proefwerk** (big), **toetsweek**. In the bovenbouw the **PTA** (programma van toetsing en afsluiting) lists all **schoolexamen (SE)** components; **centraal examen (CE)** in May; final grade = average of SE and CE. Pass rules: CE average at least 5.5 unrounded; no final grade below 4; kernvakken Nederlands, Engels and wiskunde at most one 5 (havo/vwo); limited compensation; one **herkansing** in the *tweede tijdvak*; cum laude at 8 average or above **[check yearly details on examenblad.nl]**. Outcome: **geslaagd** or **gezakt**; a geslaagd household hangs the **vlag met de schooltas** out for a few days.

Promotion: **overgaan / bevorderd** versus **blijven zitten (doubleren)**; borderline cases are a *bespreekgeval* at the *overgangsvergadering* per the school's *bevorderingsnormen*; **afstromen** (down a level) and **opstromen** (up).

### Conventions and unwritten rules
- Communication runs through **Magister** or **Somtoday** (ouderportaal plus app): cijfers, rooster, huiswerk, absenties; **ziekmelden** there or by phone before 08.15 (varies); *beter melden* when back.
- **Verlof** outside holidays only for *gewichtige omstandigheden*; form to the teamleider; over 10 days goes to the **leerplichtambtenaar**; unauthorised absence = *ongeoorloofd verzuim*. **Leerplicht** to 16, *kwalificatieplicht* to 18 without a startkwalificatie.
- **Vrijwillige ouderbijdrage** (average about €204 in vo) is genuinely voluntary; pupils cannot be excluded from activities; the oudergeleding of the **MR** approves it. Also: boekenpakket (free), laptop (often own), kluisjeshuur, werkweek, gala.
- Meetings: **ouderavond** (general information), **tienminutengesprek** (with 2 to 3 vakdocenten by booking), **mentorgesprek** (often a *driehoeksgesprek* with the pupil present). Bring written questions.
- Support ladder: mentor, then **zorgcoördinator** (also *ondersteuningscoördinator*), then vertrouwenspersoon or anti-pestcoördinator, then jeugdarts (GGD) and schoolmaatschappelijk werk. Dyslexie: a *dyslexieverklaring* gives extra time and other facilities **[school-specific]**. External paid help: *bijles*, *huiswerkbegeleiding*, *examentraining*.
- Phones: national ban on mobiles in class since 2024 **[check school rules]**.
- Register: address teachers with **u** and *meneer/mevrouw* plus achternaam; email opens *Beste* (mentor you know) or *Geachte* (unknown) and closes *Met vriendelijke groet*.
- Schoolplein and WhatsApp tone: light, practical, supportive ("Herkenbaar!", "Succes met de toetsweek"); never criticise a teacher by name in the group.
